**Learned:** `SetReadDeadline` takes an absolute `time.Time`, not a duration, and persists until changed, so an idle timeout means resetting it before every read. Set once after `Accept` it's a lifetime limit. Deadlines unblock a stuck `Read`; context cancellation doesn't (relevant for graceful shutdown).
